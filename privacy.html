Personal-use OAuth integration

Privacy Policy

Application: rclone-nuc
Last updated: October 18, 2026

Overview

rclone-nuc is a personal, non-commercial Google Drive integration used by its owner to back up, restore, and manage files using the open-source rclone tool on a self-managed computer. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Google account access

With the account owner's consent, rclone uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access Google Drive. Depending on the scope granted and the rclone operations the owner chooses, this may include listing, reading, creating, updating, and deleting Drive files. The purpose of this access is exclusively to operate the owner's backup and restore workflow.

How data is handled

  • Drive API requests and file transfers are made by rclone on the owner's machine, directly with Google services.
  • OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration, subject to the owner's device security and configuration.
  • Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Client-side encryption can be configured using rclone crypt.
  • The project does not operate a separate server, database, or service that collects Google Drive content or OAuth tokens.
  • Data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with unrelated third parties. Google processes Drive data as the selected storage provider.

Retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them or changes the backup retention settings. Local authorization tokens can be removed from the rclone configuration. The owner may revoke Google account access at any time in their Google account's third-party connections.
